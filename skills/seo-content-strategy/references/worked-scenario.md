# Worked scenario — seo-content-strategy

## Appropriate request
Task: build a content cluster around NFC business reviews.

## Near-miss request
Not this task: validate a sitemap XML file.

## Domain-specific procedure
Start with real questions, unique experience and product facts; map search intent, what evidence is needed, candidate article titles, primary/subtopic hierarchy and intentional links. Produce one evidence-based brief with audience, question set, differentiated thesis, claims needing sources and CTA.

## Required output
Topic cluster, content briefs, review owner, fact source ledger and freshness triggers.

Demonstrative scenario only. Do not claim this scenario has been executed by a model.
